OUR STORY

Experience the Art of Italian Hospitality

Step into La Alta Vita, established in 2016 under the Avolta brand, and discover the art of authentic Italian dining at Terminal 1, opposite Gate 12. Every plate is thoughtfully prepared with premium ingredients, traditional recipes, and a passion for exceptional taste. With elegant surroundings, attentive service, and unforgettable flavours, La Alta Vita turns every visit into a truly memorable experience.
